The trip

What you'll actually do

Samburu feels like a different country from the Mara: the Ewaso Ng'iro river runs green through grey-gold scrub, and the animals here have adapted to heat that keeps the day-trip crowds away. Gerenuk stand on their hind legs to feed, reticulated giraffe move in loose towers, and Grevy's zebra — thinner-striped and taller than their plains cousins — graze the river line.

It is also one of Kenya's best leopard addresses; the riverine forest holds them and our guides know the territories. From Lake Nakuru on the return leg you get rhino, flamingo and a short, easy drive back to Nairobi — ideal if you have a morning flight on day five.

Itinerary

Day-by-day itinerary

Written the way you will actually live each day.

Five hours north through Nanyuki country, crossing the equator (yes, we stop). Lunch at camp, afternoon drive along the Ewaso Ng'iro: elephant at the river, the first giraffe, and a sundowner on a granite kopje.
Early start for the leopard territories. Breakfast in the field, rest through the heat — Samburu's midday is genuinely hot — then a long afternoon. Look for the Special Five along the river line and vulture roosts on the olives.
Five and a half hours south with lunch at a roadhouse. Afternoon lakeshore drive: white and black rhino, waterbuck, and the yellow-barked acacia woodland where we track on foot with a ranger if you would like to.
Sunrise drive for the flamingo shoreline and the rhino sanctuary, then three hours to Nairobi. Afternoon free — Giraffe Centre, Karen Blixen Museum, or a very good coffee in Westlands.
Transfer to the airport timed to your flight. Flying to the coast instead? We book the connection and handle the bags.

No surprises

What's included — and what isn't

Line by line, before you pay a shilling. Your quotation repeats this list with real prices attached.

Included

  • All park entrance fees (Mara, Amboseli, Tsavo, Samburu, Nakuru as per routing)
  • Private 4x4 Land Cruiser with pop-up roof, fridge and charging points — never shared
  • English/Swahili-speaking Kenyan guide, KPSGA certified, on safari with you the whole way
  • Airport and hotel transfers in Nairobi, in daylight, in our own vehicles
  • Full-board accommodation as per the itinerary (breakfast, lunch, dinner)
  • Unlimited bottled drinking water in the vehicle
  • All government taxes and service charges
  • 24/7 WhatsApp support from our Nairobi desk while you travel

Not included

  • International flights to and from Nairobi (we can advise on routes and fares)
  • Kenya eTA — the electronic travel authorisation, applied for online before departure
  • Travel and medical insurance (required, and genuinely worth having)
  • Visas, vaccinations and anti-malaria tablets
  • Hot-air balloon safari, unless it is listed in your itinerary as included
  • Premium drinks, laundry and minibar items at the lodges
  • Tips for your guide (never expected — $10–20 pp/day is the common range if you wish)
  • Personal expenses, souvenirs and any activity not described in the itinerary

Park fees change on 1 July each year — we quote with the current rates and honour them for 30 days.

The trip

What you'll actually do

Watamu's reef is the reason people come: a curve of coral gardens inside a national marine park, shallow enough to snorkel and clear enough to see the bottom at four metres. Green turtles nest on the beach in front of it, and they feed along it — you will very likely meet one.

The 'blue safari' is the local name for the boat day. We leave the lagoon, work the dolphin zone offshore, tie up at a sandbank for lunch, then cut back through the Mida Creek mangroves where the crab traps hang and the light turns green. Everything runs on the tide — we will tell you at breakfast which half of the day is glassier.

Every routing is a starting point. Tell us your dates and pace and we will adjust it before you pay anything.

Itinerary

The day, hour by hour

A full-day departure — half-day versions cut the lunch stop and Mida leg.

07:30

Hotel pickup

Minibus from your hotel to Watamu jetty — five to fifteen minutes depending on where you are staying.

08:00

Board and briefing

Life jackets, snorkel fitting, tide briefing. We clear the lagoon and turn south.

09:00

Dolphin zone

The channel between Watamu and Malindi holds resident spinner pods. Engine off, drift, listen — this is where most sightings happen.

10:30

Turtle Island reef

Snorkel over the coral gardens inside the marine park. Turtle sightings are near-daily; reef sharks and rays are common at the drop-off.

12:30

Lunch on the sandbank

Grilled seafood or chicken, chapati, tropical fruit, cold drinks — served on the sand with nobody else on it.

14:00

Mida Creek

The boat narrows into the mangroves: crab traps, kingfishers, fish eagles and a mud-crab fishing demo if the tide is right.

16:00

Return

Back on the beach by 16:00–16:30, transfer to your hotel. Sunset swim, dinner reservation already sorted if you want it.

No surprises

What's included — and what isn't

Line by line, before you pay a shilling. Your quotation repeats this list with real prices attached.

Included

  • Traditional wooden dhow or fiberglass boat with shade canopy and safety gear
  • Licensed skipper plus a marine guide who knows the tides and the turtle sites
  • Fuel, landing fees and all boat running costs
  • Snorkel mask, fin set and short wetsuit/rash vest (cleaned between guests)
  • Life jackets for adults and children, and a rescue ring on board
  • Drinking water, soft drinks and Swahili snacks all day
  • Full-day guests: grilled seafood or chicken lunch on the sandbank or on board
  • Hotel transfers in Watamu/Malindi (within 15 km of the jetty)

Not included

  • Watamu or Malindi Marine Park entry fee — approx. $17 per adult per day, paid at the gate
  • Alcoholic drinks and premium beverages
  • PADI/SSI scuba courses and certified dives (we can book these separately)
  • Towels, sunscreen and personal snorkel masks if you prefer your own
  • Tips for the crew — never expected; $5–10 per person is customary
  • Photography packages and underwater camera hire
  • Travel and medical insurance (including water-sports cover)
  • Anything not listed as included above

Park fees change on 1 July each year — we quote with the current rates and honour them for 30 days.
